LabGenie App

Terms of use and privacy

This page explains what the LabGenie app does with information from your health record when you connect it through MyChart. Please read it before you connect.

Last updated: October 28, 2026

In short

The short version

  • LabGenie is a research tool from Florida State University. It is only for people taking part in the LabGenie research study.
  • With your permission, it reads your lab results and some related information from your health record. It cannot change anything in your record.
  • It explains your results in plain language and suggests questions to ask your care team.
  • It does not give medical advice, diagnoses, or treatment.
  • Your information is never sold and never used for advertising.
  • You can disconnect at any time.

About

What LabGenie is

LabGenie is a web application developed by researchers at Florida State University (FSU) as part of a research study approved by FSU's Institutional Review Board. It helps people understand their laboratory test results.

LabGenie is available only to people who have agreed to take part in the study through its consent process. Being invited to connect LabGenie does not change your care, and choosing not to connect will not affect your care in any way.

LabGenie is a research tool. It is not a medical device and is not a substitute for advice from your doctor or care team.

Your information

What LabGenie reads from your health record

When you sign in to MyChart and approve the connection, LabGenie can read the following from your health record:

  • Your laboratory test results and lab reports
  • Your health conditions (your problem list and diagnoses from visits)
  • Your medication orders
  • Your visits
  • Basic details such as gender, race, and ethnicity

LabGenie only reads information. It cannot add to, change, or delete anything in your health record, and it cannot send messages to your care team.

After you connect, LabGenie may check for new results when you open the app, so you do not have to sign in to MyChart every time. This continues until you disconnect or the connection expires.

How your information is used

  • To show you your results, how they have changed over time, and plain-language explanations
  • To suggest questions you may want to discuss with your care team
  • For the research study you agreed to, as described in your consent form, including improving LabGenie

Some explanations are written by artificial intelligence (AI). The AI service runs inside FSU's secure cloud environment, and your information is not used to train AI models.

Your information is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with companies for marketing.

Privacy

Who can see your information

  • You, when you sign in to LabGenie
  • Members of the FSU research team who are approved to work on the study

Like all university research, the study may be reviewed by FSU's Institutional Review Board and other groups that oversee research. We will also share information if required by law.

How your information is protected

  • Information is stored in FSU's secure Microsoft Azure cloud environment, on private networks that are not open to the internet.
  • Information is encrypted while it is sent and while it is stored.
  • The permission that lets LabGenie check for new results is stored encrypted, and is never shown in your web browser.
  • Only approved research team members can access study data.

How long your information is kept

Study information is kept for the duration of the study phase of the research project.

You can ask for a copy of all the information LabGenie has stored about you, or ask us to delete it, at any time by emailing zhe@fsu.edu. If you leave the study and ask us to delete your information, it will not be kept.

Your choices

Disconnecting and leaving the study

You can stop LabGenie from reading your health record at any time, in either of these ways:

  • In MyChart, go to your account settings and remove LabGenie from your connected apps.
  • Contact the research team (details below) and ask us to disconnect you.

Once you disconnect, LabGenie can no longer get new information from your health record. Taking part in the study is voluntary, and you can leave at any time without affecting your care.

Medical advice and emergencies

LabGenie does not diagnose conditions or recommend treatment. Always talk with your doctor or care team about your results and before making any change to your care.

If you think you are having a medical emergency, call 911.

Changes to this page

We may update this page. When we do, we will change the "last updated" date at the top. If a change affects how your information is used, the research team will tell you.

Contact

Questions

Dr. Zhe He leads the LabGenie project. For questions about LabGenie, this page, or the study, email zhe@fsu.edu.

For questions about your rights as a research participant, you can contact the FSU Institutional Review Board, as listed in your consent form.
